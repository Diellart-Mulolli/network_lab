RFC 6811 validity of Orange's routes (origin AS12479) in each phase
  Valid = a ROA covers the prefix, allows its length and names the origin AS
  Invalid = covered by a ROA, but none matches (wrong AS or longer than maxLength)
  NotFound = no ROA covers the prefix

  route                    phase 0       phase 1       phase 2       phase 3
  85.48.0.0/12            NotFound       Invalid         Valid         Valid
  85.48.12.0/24           NotFound       Invalid       Invalid         Valid
  90.163.112.0/24         NotFound       Invalid       Invalid         Valid
  62.36.0.0/16            NotFound       Invalid         Valid         Valid
  62.36.13.0/24           NotFound       Invalid       Invalid         Valid

  phase 0  before: no ROA for these prefixes (the account had never created one)
  phase 1  14:20 UTC: attacker's ROAs with origin AS49581 (the two /12s, plus the earlier /16)
  phase 2  ~18:00 UTC: Orange regains the account: AS12479 ROAs, maxLength = prefix length
  phase 3  8 Jan 16:00 UTC: Orange raises the maxLength to 24

Why only networks that do route origin validation (ROV) lost the routes
  an ROV network drops 'Invalid' routes; a network without ROV accepts every route.
  in phase 1 every Orange route is Invalid, so ROV networks drop it and the others keep it;
  Kentik: when a route is evaluated as Invalid, its propagation is cut in half or more.

What the same ROAs allow the attacker (phase 1)
  announcing 85.48.0.0/12 from AS49581: Valid  (the attacker's own ROA makes his route Valid)
  announcing 85.48.0.0/12 from AS12479: Invalid

Why some routes stayed Invalid until 8 January (phase 2)
  85.48.12.0/24      /24 longer than maxLength 12 or 16: Invalid; with maxLength 24: Valid
  90.163.112.0/24    /24 longer than maxLength 12 or 16: Invalid; with maxLength 24: Valid
  62.36.13.0/24      /24 longer than maxLength 12 or 16: Invalid; with maxLength 24: Valid

Size of the loss (Kentik / APNIC, 3-4 January 2024)
  globally routed routes of AS12479: 9,200 -> 7,400 = -1,800 (19.6 %)
  unique IPv4 addresses originated:  7 M -> 5 M = -2 M (28.6 %)
